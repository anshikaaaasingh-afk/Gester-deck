Gesture Deck

Present with your hands. Your camera feed is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

  • Swipe an open palm left or right to change slides
  • Point your index finger for a laser pointer
  • Pinch and hold to zoom into a spot
  • Fist for 1 second to lock or unlock gestures

Keys: arrows to navigate, C calibration view, L load .md slides, F fullscreen
